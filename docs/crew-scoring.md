# Crew balance: role fit, not a power ranking

All 48 eligible members have explicit scores across leadership, swordsmanship, navigation, cooking, medicine, music, marksmanship and wildcard utility. Scores range from 0 to 100. A strong fighter can be a terrible fit for a particular job. Wildcard slots share a utility score.

Players see a role's exact points only after committing the character. The pre-assignment buttons and rules contain no score table. Assigned berths and completed crews show their points. Scores remain server-validated; these open-source game rules are not a promise of cheat-proof secrecy.

Law is the top doctor at 100, Marco follows at 98 and Chopper at 97. The official character pages identify [Marco as a ship's doctor](https://one-piece.com/character/marco/index.html) and [Law as the Surgeon of Death](https://one-piece.com/character/law/index.html). Their numeric ordering is an editorial game choice, not an official ranking.

Leadership now rewards established captains, including Buggy, Big Mom and Shiki. Shiki receives useful navigation fit below trained navigators. Hancock gets credit as a practical cook; Big Mom gets a strong creative cooking fit based on food-related crew utility. These are deliberately playful judgments, not claims that either character has Sanji's professional training. Strength, intelligence and versatile abilities can help in secondary roles without making every role excellent. Low scores are reserved for clear mismatches, such as Zoro navigating or Luffy practising medicine.

Draws retain their existing weighted randomness, no duplicates and introduction gates. Nothing forces a high score or a near miss. The ceiling remains 999, and 1000 remains impossible. An ideal distinct lineup still reaches the ceiling; 990+ requires very specific draws and assignments. A deterministic 20,000-run regression simulation with random assignments guards against accidental top-tier inflation; it is not an exact probability for optimal play.

Existing saved lineups are preserved and evaluated with the current scores. Personal-best comparisons also use current scores instead of stale stored totals. No account or progress migration is needed.
