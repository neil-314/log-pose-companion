Log Pose Companion is a free, unofficial One Piece fan project built with AI assistance. You can explore as a guest. Email sign-in is optional and is used to save a private logbook.

Guest play and browser storage

You do not need to provide an email to explore. Display settings and tour preferences may be saved in your browser. Use the logbook export if you want a portable backup of your progress.

Email sign-in and saved progress

Email sign-in is optional. When enabled, Supabase Auth processes your email address and one-time verification code to authenticate your account; your email is not used for marketing messages. After you verify your email, the app can save your progress, rankings, ratings, revisits, notes, quiz records, and crew-game records in an account-linked logbook stored with Supabase. Account data is protected by account-level access controls. Sign-in uses a session cookie to keep you authenticated. Email delivery is still being configured, so sign-in may not yet be available.

Community and sharing

Your email and private logbook are not shown publicly. Some Harbour features let you opt in to a public profile or publish posts and replies. The information you choose to share there can be seen by other visitors. Avoid including private or sensitive details in public content.

Analytics and service providers

The main logbook page uses Vercel Web Analytics for page views. Before a page-view URL is sent, this app removes its query string and fragment. Vercel says Web Analytics does not use cookies and uses a visitor hash that expires after one day; aggregated reports may include referrers and general browser, device, and location categories. See Vercel’s analytics privacy details.

The site is hosted by Vercel and uses Supabase for optional authentication and account storage. These providers process information needed to operate their services under their own privacy notices. The site does not sell personal information or use advertising.

Feedback and privacy requests

The feedback page prepares a report for you to copy or download; it does not send the report automatically. For a question or a request to access or delete account data, email the project maintainer. Please do not include passwords or sign-in codes.
